# Shopping list: pilot rig (1 camera, 4 plates)

Everything needed to build **one recording box** that films **4 plates of exactly 10 bees** at once, in
colour, under constant light. The design is in [README.md](README.md); printable parts are in [`cad/`](cad/).

How to read the links:
- **Product** links point to one specific product.
- **Search** links open an AliExpress or Amazon search with the right words. Pick a listing that matches the
  *Must have* column, preferably with many orders and good reviews. Listings change too often to pin one.

Prices are rough (November 2026), in shekels, and should be checked when ordering. Pilot total:
**≈ ₪2,700–4,000**, about half of it the camera and lens.

## Before you order

1. **A1**: confirm with the seller which variant the price is for, and what's in the box.
2. **A2**: confirm the lens is C-mount, 2/3" and has locking screws; write down its filter thread and length.
3. **D1**: order the glass after printing one test plate, so you can check the groove with the real glass.
4. Print and test **one plate** with a few bees before printing all four (see [README → Pilot](README.md#pilot-checklist)).
